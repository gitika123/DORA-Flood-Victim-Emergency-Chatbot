## 📋 About

**DORA** (Disaster Operations Response Assistant) is an intelligent chatbot designed to bridge the communication gap between flood victims and emergency officials during critical situations. Built using IBM Watson Assistant, DORA provides instant access to emergency services, toll-free numbers, and vital information when victims need help the most.

### 🎯 Problem Statement

During natural disasters like floods, victims often struggle to reach emergency services quickly. Traditional communication channels can be overwhelmed, leading to delayed responses and increased casualties. DORA addresses this critical gap by providing an accessible, 24/7 AI-powered interface that connects victims with the right resources instantly.

### ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Assistance**: Built on IBM Watson Assistant for intelligent, context-aware responses
- 🆘 **Emergency Service Directory**: Quick access to toll-free numbers and emergency contacts
- 🌐 **Web-Based Interface**: Accessible from any device with internet connectivity
- ⚡ **Instant Response**: Real-time assistance without waiting for human operators
- 📱 **Mobile-Friendly**: Responsive design works seamlessly on smartphones and tablets
- 🔄 **24/7 Availability**: Always available, even when emergency call centers are overwhelmed

## 💡 Usage Example

When a flood victim needs assistance, they can simply message DORA:

**User**: "I need food supplies"

**DORA**: "I understand you need food assistance. Here's the toll-free number for emergency food services: [Number]. You can also contact [Organization Name] at [Contact Info]."
